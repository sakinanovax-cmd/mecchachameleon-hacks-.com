What Is Meccha Chameleon?

Meccha Chameleon is a multiplayer hide-and-seek painting game from Japanese indie creators Lemorion_1224 and Haganeiro. Hiders paint their bodies to blend into maps; seekers hunt them down before time runs out. Players search for Meccha Chameleon gameplay, Meccha Chameleon Steam, Meccha Chameleon camouflage tips, and Meccha Chameleon prop hunt comparisons because the loop mixes artistic skill with ranking pressure.
